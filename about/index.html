RankPro Academy is Calicut's academy for SSC, Railway and Kerala PSC exam coaching — built around one idea: a rank is the result of a method, followed consistently for twelve months.

Who we are

Your rank, our mission

We run offline, classroom-based coaching out of Calicut for SSC, Railway (RRB) and Kerala PSC exams. Every batch follows the same structured 12-month program — six months to complete the syllabus, six more to convert it into exam-day performance.

SSC: CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD RRB: NTPC, Group D, ALP, JE Kerala PSC
Our approach

The RankPro Method

Five phases, run on repeat, until the score holds under exam conditions.

Learn

Complete the syllabus and build concepts from the ground up.

Practice

PYQs, topic tests and regular exercises to make concepts stick.

Test

Full-length mock tests run under real exam-like conditions.

Analyse

Identify mistakes, weak areas and time-management issues.

Improve

Revise, practise, retest — and watch the score move.
