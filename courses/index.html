Central Government

SSC Exams

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is a central government recruitment body that conducts exams to select staff for various posts across central government departments and organisations. Clearing an SSC exam leads to a stable government job in central offices and departments.

  • SSC CGL — Graduate level posts
  • SSC CHSL — Higher Secondary (12th) level
  • SSC MTS — 10th level
  • SSC GD — Constable posts in central armed police forces
Indian Railways

Railway (RRB) Exams

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is the central government body that conducts exams and recruitment for various posts across Indian Railways — one of the country's largest employers. Candidates with a range of educational qualifications, from 10th and 12th to ITI, Diploma and Degree, can apply for different railway posts through RRB exams.

  • RRB NTPC — Non-Technical Popular Categories
  • RRB Group D — various Level-1 posts
  • RRB ALP — Assistant Loco Pilot
  • RRB JE — Junior Engineer
State Government

Kerala PSC

Kerala PSC recruits for state government departments and public sector posts — Kerala's own route into stable, local government employment, covering everything from clerical to uniformed and degree-level posts.

  • LDC, LGS, VFA
  • 10th Level Preliminary
  • Degree Level Preliminary
  • Degree Level Mains
  • Uniform post exams
